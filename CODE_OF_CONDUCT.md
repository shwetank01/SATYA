# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make taking part in SATYA a harassment-free experience for everyone. That holds regardless of:
- age, body size or visible or invisible disability;
- ethnicity or caste;
- sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, or sexual identity and orientation;
- level of experience or education;
- socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion or language.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:
- being kind and patient, especially with people new to the project or to English;
- giving and accepting feedback on the work, not the person;
- owning our mistakes and learning from them;
- focusing on what is best for the people SATYA protects.

Behaviour we do not accept:
- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention;
- trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing others' private information without their explicit permission. This includes real scam victims' details in issues or test data.
- any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through GitHub (**Security → Report a vulnerability** is private), or by contacting the repository owner. Every report is reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code. They may also ban a contributor temporarily or permanently, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
